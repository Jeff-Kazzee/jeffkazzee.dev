Zo 101 is a free field guide for people learning their way around Zo Computer. It covers the practical first questions: memory, the browser, hosting, automations, personas, integrations, recipe packs, and how to judge AI output without pretending the tool is magic.

You can search the site, browse by topic, and use it without a login. Read a guide, copy a recipe, then go build inside your own Zo workspace at [zocomputer101.wiki](https://www.zocomputer101.wiki/).
