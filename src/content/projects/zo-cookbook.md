Zo Cookbook collects more than a thousand app ideas, space configurations, automations, and prompts for Zo Computer users. You can browse by category, tab, search, or discover mode.

Recipes stay public, execution stays private. Pick one, copy the brief, and run it inside your own Zo. The cookbook provides the instructions. Your Zo does the work.

The site accepts community submissions and publishes additions through a changelog and what's-new feed.
