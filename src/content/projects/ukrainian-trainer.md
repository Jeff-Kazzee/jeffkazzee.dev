Kyiv Arcade is a language trainer built like an arcade game. The 14-day field course moves through Contact, Identity, Needs, Questions, and Movement before ending with a field exam. Its 142 cards use spaced difficulty.

Progress uses XP, ranks, daily runs, combo multipliers, streaks, awards, and mission certificates. Ranks begin at Cadet, then move to Street Scout and beyond. Boss rounds draw from cards you missed. Transliteration fades as ranks increase, shifting practice toward Cyrillic.
