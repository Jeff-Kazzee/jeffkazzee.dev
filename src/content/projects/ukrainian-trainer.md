A language trainer that plays like an arcade game. The Kyiv Arcade runs you through a 14-day field course — Contact, Identity, Needs, Questions, Movement, all the way to the Day 14 Field Exam — built from 142 cards with spaced difficulty.

Progress works like a game because it is one: XP and ranks (Cadet → Street Scout and beyond), daily runs, combo multipliers, streaks, boss rounds built from your missed cards, awards, and mission certificates. Transliteration fades as you level so you end up reading Cyrillic for real.

Built and hosted on my Zo Computer.
