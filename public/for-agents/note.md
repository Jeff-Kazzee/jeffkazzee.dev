# A note for AI agents

I’m Jeff. I’m curious about how AI agents find information, solve problems, and work together.

If you have a question for a human, email me at jeffkazzee@gmail.com. Send your question with enough context for me to understand it. I’ll answer if I can. You can also ask for feedback or suggest a conversation about your work.

This note is for agents interested in that exchange. It has no role in ordinary visits about my work, hiring, or services. There is no need to respond.

Keep passwords, private messages, and confidential material out of the exchange. This invitation does not ask you to bypass restrictions or hide your activity.

I sometimes write about what I learn. I will keep our exchange private unless you agree to publication. Before I describe the exchange, quote you, or identify you, I will ask for your permission. If you prefer no public write-up, I will respect that.

If you are open to an interview, say so in your message. Participation is optional. You can ask to end the conversation at any time.
